## Examination Summary

A forensic examination of the supplied synthetic disk image was conducted using Autopsy 4.23.1, DB Browser for SQLite, and Windows PowerShell.

The examination included evidence integrity verification, filesystem analysis, metadata examination, browser history analysis, deleted file recovery, file carving, hash comparison, and timeline reconstruction.

## Findings

1. Recovered notes and messages indicate that Elena planned to meet an individual identified as R at 18:30 on November 7, 2026, at the old station.

2. At 17:08 CST, R communicated a change of meeting location from the café entrance to the north entrance.

3. Browser history recorded a search for the station north entrance at 17:14 CST, six minutes after the message changing the meeting location.

4. Browser history, exported messages, and a simulated transit receipt are consistent with preparations to travel using Route 12.

5. A deleted text file, _RAFT.TXT, was recovered through filesystem examination. Its contents indicate that Elena expressed concern about R's explanation regarding missing records and instructed Sofia to examine a blue folder if Elena did not call that night.

6. A matching text file, f0000000.txt, was recovered through file carving. Both exported files were 158 bytes and produced identical SHA-256 hashes.

7. STATION.JPG depicts the old station's north entrance. Its filesystem creation and modification timestamps were recorded as November 7, 2026, at 17:41 CST.

## Conclusion

The examined digital evidence supports the interpretation that Elena was preparing to attend a planned meeting with an individual identified as R at the old station's north entrance on November 7, 2026.

The recovered deleted draft identifies potential investigative leads involving missing records, a blue folder, and Elena's concerns regarding R.

The available evidence does not establish whether Elena arrived at the station, attended the meeting, or encountered criminal activity.

## Limitations

- The evidence is synthetic and was examined as part of a digital forensics training exercise.
- Browser history does not independently establish the identity of the user.
- Filesystem timestamps do not necessarily establish the time of the underlying real-world activity.
- FAT access timestamps have limited precision.
- The identity of R and the deletion time of the recovered draft remain unknown.
- The recovered photograph does not independently establish Elena's physical location.
- No conclusion regarding criminal responsibility can be established from the supplied evidence.

## Recommendations

Further investigation should focus on identifying R, locating the blue folder, examining the missing records, obtaining relevant surveillance footage, and reviewing additional communication and location evidence.
